Welcome!

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate at the Gies College of Business, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, specializing in Information Systems, Operations, Supply Chain, and Analytics (IOSA). I am fortunate to be advised by Professors Sridhar Seshadri and Xin Chen.

My research focuses on data-driven decision-making to advance social welfare in underserved communities, with particular interests in sustainable operations, nonprofit operations, and healthcare. Through close engagement with practitioners, I identify operational challenges and study how organizations can allocate scarce resources and design services more efficiently, sustainably, and equitably.

Methodologically, I use optimization and machine learning to develop rigorous algorithms that are implementable in practice. I complement this work with field experiments and operational data to evaluate their performance in real-world settings.

Before joining UIUC, I received an M.A. in Business Analytics from the University of California, Irvine, an M.A. in Financial Mathematics from Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey, and a B.S. in Financial Engineering from the University of International Business and Economics.

Beyond research, I am also a professional artist.
